ECON 105A — Closed Economy in the Long-Run

Closed Economy Graph

Practice graphical analysis of the loanable funds market: identify equilibrium, apply a shock, and trace its effects on the interest rate and on equilibrium savings and investment.

Instructions

The loanable funds diagram has the real interest rate r on the vertical axis and savings / investment on the horizontal axis. Investment I(r) = I0 − I1r always slopes downward. National savings is either fixed (S = Y − C(Y−T) − G, Section 4.5) or rises with the interest rate (S(r) = Y − C(Y−T, r) − G, Section 4.6). The savings model is randomly assigned each problem and shown in the badge above the graph.

The original equilibrium is marked on the graph from the start. You will work through the following steps:

  1. Get a shock. The lab will randomly draw one of: G↑, T↓, T↑, I↑, or I↓.
  2. Shift the correct curve. Choose which curve shifts and in which direction.
  3. Predict the direction of r and equilibrium S/I. Will each rise, fall, or stay unchanged?
  4. Mark the new equilibrium. Drag the crosshair (or use the arrow keys) to where you think the new intersection lies, then commit.
  5. Compare original and new equilibria. Read off the changes in r and S = I, and identify any crowding out.
