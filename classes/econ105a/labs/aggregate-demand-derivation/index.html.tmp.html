ECON 105A — Aggregate Demand

Deriving Aggregate Demand

Drag the price level P from high to low and watch how the LM curve shifts, the IS-LM equilibrium moves, and a new point pins on the AD curve every two units of P.

Instructions

The economy is fixed — the only thing you change is the price level P.

  1. The slider starts at P = 10 and the first snapshot (point A) is already pinned.
  2. Drag the slider down. Each time P drops by two (to 8, 6, 4, 2), a new labeled snapshot (B, C, D, E) is pinned. On the money market and IS-LM panels, the corresponding MS and LM lines stay drawn in gray with the point's letter. On the AD panel, the labeled (Y, P) point appears.
  3. The smooth AD curve is drawn underneath the snapshot dots from the start — as you drag, you watch the equilibrium move along it.
  4. When the slider reaches P = 2, all five snapshots are pinned and the lab is complete.

Key idea: Each pinned point is a full IS-LM equilibrium for a particular value of P. The AD curve is the locus of all such equilibria as P varies — the hyperbola Y = α + β/P.
