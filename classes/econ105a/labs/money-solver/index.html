ECON 105A — Money

Money Solver

Compute the money multiplier, money supply, currency, deposits, and reserves from the three primitives of the money-supply model: the monetary base, the currency-deposit ratio, and the reserve-deposit ratio.

Instructions

In this lab, you will practice computing the money supply from its three primitives: the monetary base, the currency-deposit ratio, and the reserve-deposit ratio. You'll see how a small monetary base can create a much larger money supply through fractional-reserve banking.

You will be asked to answer the following questions:

  1. Money multiplier. Compute m from cr and rr.
  2. Money supply. Combine the multiplier with the monetary base to find M.
  3. Currency. Use M and the currency-deposit ratio to find C.
  4. Deposits. Find D, the part of the money supply that is not currency.
  5. Reserves. Find R, the amount that banks keep on hand rather than lending out.

For each step, enter your answer and click Check Answer. If your answer is incorrect, a conceptual Hint, the relevant Formula, and a full worked Solution become available in turn.
