ECON 105A — Closed Economy in the Long-Run

Circular Flow Builder

Build the circular flow diagram from scratch, verify each box's money-in equals money-out, then derive the chapter's central identity: SPR + SPU = S = I.

Instructions

The diagram has five boxes — Households, Government, Firms, Markets for Factors of Production, Financial Markets, and Markets for Goods and Services — connected by nine arrows showing flows of money.

You will work through three phases:

  1. Phase 1 — Build: place the nine flow labels (Consumption, Investment, Taxes, etc.) onto the correct arrows. You can drag the chips with the mouse, or pick an arrow from the dropdown next to each chip with the keyboard.
  2. Phase 2 — Verify: inspect each box and confirm that the money flowing in equals the money flowing out.
  3. Phase 3 — Derive: use the box identities you just verified to derive the equations for private saving, public saving, and the equilibrium condition S = I.

The lab works equally well with mouse drag-and-drop or the keyboard dropdowns — pick whichever you prefer.

Income flows from the factor market to households; consumption flows from households to the goods market; firms earn revenue and pay it back out as factor payments; the government collects taxes and spends/saves; the financial market channels saving into investment.
