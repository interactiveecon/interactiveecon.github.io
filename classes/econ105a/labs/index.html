Closed Economy in the Long-Run

Circular Flow Builder

Build the circular flow diagram from scratch, verify that each box's money-in equals money-out, and derive the chapter's central identity: private saving plus public saving equals national saving equals investment.

Unemployment

Unemployment — Steady-State Flow Simulator

Watch the labor market converge to its steady-state unemployment rate. Simulate monthly flows between employment and unemployment, vary the separation and job-finding rates, and see that u* = s/(s+f) emerges from the dynamics regardless of where you start.
Open Lab →

Unemployment — Matching Model

Explore the matching model of unemployment. Adjust productivity, the labor force, the job-separation rate, and the wage; see how labor market tightness, employment, and the unemployment rate respond, and decompose total unemployment into frictional and rationing components.

Inflation

Inflation Solver

Use the Quantity Theory of Money to solve for the missing macro variable, apply the Fisher equation to find the missing nominal or real interest rate, then interpret what the result implies about long-run money neutrality or the Fisher effect.

Ex Ante vs. Ex Post Real Interest Rates

Step into the shoes of a lender and then a borrower on the same one-year loan. See how unexpected inflation drives a wedge between planned and realized real returns, then run 1,000 random inflation draws to see the distribution of realized real rates.

Aggregate Demand

IS-LM Solver

Derive the IS curve from goods-market equilibrium, the LM curve from money-market equilibrium, and solve the two together for the short-run equilibrium interest rate and output.
Open Lab →

IS-LM Graph

Apply a random shock to the economy, then prescribe the fiscal or monetary policy that fully offsets it. Watch Investment, the Keynesian Cross, the Money Market, and IS-LM all update together.
Open Lab →

Aggregate Demand Solver

Build the aggregate demand curve from its IS-LM foundations. Solve the IS curve for r, solve the LM curve for r with P kept symbolic, then set IS = LM and derive Y as a function of P.
Open Lab →

Deriving Aggregate Demand

Trace the AD curve point by point by varying the price level. See how a change in P shifts the LM curve, changes equilibrium (Y, r), and plots a new point on the AD diagram — building up the AD hyperbola from three IS-LM equilibria.
Open Lab →

Aggregate Demand Prediction

Read a news-style scenario about a shock to G, T, c0, I0, P, or M, then predict how IS, LM, and AD respond. Build the causal mechanism through the money market and move the unlocked slider to see what actually happens.
Open Lab →

Policy Classifier

Read a news-style policy headline and classify it: fiscal or monetary, expansionary or contractionary, which curve shifts and how, what happens to AD, and what happens to the equilibrium interest rate r in the money market.
Open Lab →

What Went Wrong?

Each question shows a causal chain with exactly one broken link. Find the first step where the logic goes wrong. Covers fiscal policy, monetary policy through the money market and LM curve, IS-curve mechanics, and aggregate demand.
