ECON 105A — Unemployment

Identify the Shock

Diagnose which shock — to productivity, the wage, the labor force, or the separation rate — caused the labor market to move from one equilibrium to another.

Instructions

You'll see a sequence of 8 labor market diagrams. In each one, the economy starts at one equilibrium (dashed curves, hollow dot) and moves to a new equilibrium (solid curves, filled dot). Your job: identify which shock caused the change.

There are four possible shock types, each with an "up" or "down" direction:

  1. Productivity (A). Shifts labor demand. Also changes the demand curve's slope.
  2. Wage (W). Shifts labor demand. Parallel shift only.
  3. Labor force (H). Shifts labor supply. The vertical H line moves with it.
  4. Separation rate (s). Shifts labor supply. The H line does NOT move.

For each problem, select the shock you think caused the change and click Check Answer. If you're stuck, use the Hint button — it tells you which curve moved without revealing the specific shock.
