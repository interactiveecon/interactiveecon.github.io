ECON 105A — Aggregate Demand

IS-LM Solver

Derive the IS curve from goods-market equilibrium, the LM curve from money-market equilibrium, and then solve the two together for the short-run equilibrium interest rate and output.

Instructions

You are given a consumption function with an interest-rate term, an investment function, fiscal variables G and T, a real money-demand function L(r,Y) = MDY·Y − MDR·r, and the nominal money supply M and price level P. Work through four steps to find the IS-LM equilibrium.

You will be asked to answer the following questions:

  1. The IS curve. Express the goods-market equilibrium output as a linear function of r. Enter the intercept and the (positive) coefficient on r.
  2. The LM curve. Express the money-market equilibrium output as a linear function of r. Enter the intercept and the coefficient on r.
  3. Equilibrium interest rate r*. Set IS = LM and solve for r.
  4. Equilibrium output Y*. Plug r* back into either curve.

For each step, enter your answer and click Check Answer. A short Hint is always available; once you have submitted an attempt, you can also reveal a full worked Solution. The next step unlocks as soon as you submit (right or wrong).

A short-run economy with both a goods market and a money market. The interest rate r is expressed in percent.
