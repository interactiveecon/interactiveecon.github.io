ECON 105A — Aggregate Demand

Aggregate Demand Solver

Build the aggregate demand curve from its IS-LM foundations. Solve the IS curve for r, solve the LM curve for r (with P kept symbolic), then set IS = LM and solve for Y as a function of P.

Instructions

You are given a consumption function with an interest-rate term, an investment function, fiscal variables G and T, a real money-demand function L(r,Y) = MDY·Y − MDR·r, and the nominal money supply M. The price level P is deliberately left as a variable — that is what lets us trace out the AD curve.

You will be asked to answer the following questions:

  1. The IS curve, solved for r. Express the goods-market equilibrium interest rate as a linear function of Y. Enter the intercept and the (positive) coefficient on Y.
  2. The LM curve, solved for r. Express the money-market equilibrium interest rate as a linear function of Y and 1/P. Enter the coefficient on Y and the numerator that sits over P.
  3. The AD curve. Set IS = LM and solve for Y as a function of P. The answer has the form Y = α + β/P. Enter α and β.

For each step, enter your answer and click Check Answer. A short Hint is always available; once you have submitted an attempt, you can also reveal a full worked Solution. The next step unlocks as soon as you submit (right or wrong).

Worked Example

Suppose the economy is described by:

C = 100 + 0.50 (Y − T) I = 100 − 20 r G = 50,   T = 40 L(r, Y) = 2 Y − 20 r M = 600,   P is a variable
Step 1IS curve, solved for r

Set Y = C + I + G, then group the r terms:

Y = 100 + 0.50(Y − 40) + 100 − 20r + 50 20 r = 100 − 0.50·40 + 100 + 50 − (1 − 0.50) Y 20 r = 230 − 0.50 Y r = 23020 − 0.5020 Y r = 11.50 − 0.025 Y
Step 2LM curve, solved for r

Set M/P = L(r, Y) and solve for r, keeping P symbolic:

600P = 2 Y − 20 r 20 r = 2 Y − 600P r = 220 Y − 60020·P r = 0.10 Y − 30P
Step 3AD curve

Set IS = LM (both equal r) and solve for Y:

11.50 − 0.025 Y = 0.10 Y − 30P 11.50 + 30P = (0.025 + 0.10) Y = 0.125 Y Y = 11.500.125 + 300.125·P Y = 92 + 240P

So at P = 1 the economy demands Y = 332; at P = 3, Y = 172; at P = 10, Y = 116. Plotting (Y, P) traces out the downward-sloping aggregate demand curve.
