ECON 105A — Closed Economy in the Long-Run

Closed Economy Solver

Solve a closed-economy loanable funds model: derive private savings, public savings, and national savings as functions of the interest rate, find the equilibrium interest rate, and plug it back in to recover the savings breakdown.

Instructions

You are given output Y, a consumption function C = c0 + MPC·(Y−T) − c1r, an investment function I = I0 − I1r, and fiscal variables G and T. Work through five steps to characterize the loanable-funds equilibrium.

You will be asked to answer the following questions:

  1. Private savings as a function of r. Find the intercept and the coefficient on r in SPR(r).
  2. Public savings. A single number, T − G.
  3. National savings as a function of r. Find the intercept and the coefficient on r in S(r).
  4. Equilibrium interest rate r*. Set S(r) = I(r) and solve for r (as a percent, to 2 decimals).
  5. Plug r* back in. Use your r* to recover SPR(r*), SPU, and S(r*).

For each step, enter your answer and click Check Answer. If your answer is incorrect, a conceptual Hint, the relevant Formula, and a full worked Solution become available in turn.
