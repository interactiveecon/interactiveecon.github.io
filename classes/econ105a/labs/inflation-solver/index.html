ECON 105A — Inflation

Inflation Solver

Use the Quantity Theory of Money and the Fisher equation to solve for the missing macro variable, then interpret the result.

Instructions

This lab walks you through the two identities that link the aggregate price level, the money supply, real output, and the nominal and real interest rates. Each problem gives you a set of macro variables with one number missing in each step.

You will be asked to answer the following questions:

  1. Quantity Theory of Money. Use %ΔM + %ΔV = π + %ΔY to solve for the missing variable among money growth, velocity growth, inflation, and real output growth.
  2. Fisher Equation. Use i = r + π (with the inflation rate carried over from Step 1) to solve for either the nominal or the real interest rate.
  3. Interpretation. Answer one short multiple-choice question about what the result implies in the long run.

For each step, enter your answer and click Check Answer. If your answer is incorrect, a hint, the relevant formula, and then a worked solution appear in sequence. Step 2 unlocks after Step 1, and Step 3 unlocks after Step 2.
