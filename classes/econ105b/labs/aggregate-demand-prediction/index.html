ECON 105B — Review of Aggregate Demand

Aggregate Demand Prediction

Predict what shifts (or moves along) IS, LM, and AD. Then build the mechanism through the money market and test it using the unlocked slider.

Instructions

Each scenario follows the same six steps. Practice as many as you like.

  1. Read the headline. A news-style scenario describes a shock to one of G, T, c0, I0, P, or M.
  2. Predict what happens to IS, LM, and AD — choose shift vs move along and the direction for each. Click Check predictions.
  3. Build the mechanism. Drag pills into the slots in the correct causal order (or use the keyboard: Tab to a pill, Enter or Space to select, then Tab to a slot and Enter or Space to place; Delete or Backspace to remove). Click Check mechanism.
  4. Sliders are locked until you check your predictions. The app then unlocks only the correct slider, and only in the correct direction, so you can see what actually happens.
  5. Move the unlocked slider in the allowed direction to reveal the new equilibrium on all three diagrams.
  6. Click New Scenario for another. Your running score updates after each scenario is fully checked.

Scoring: Each completed scenario is worth 2 points: 1 for predictions (all three correct on the first try) and 1 for the mechanism (chain exactly correct on the first try). The session records your cumulative score so you can stop whenever you like.
