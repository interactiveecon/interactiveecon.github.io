ECON 105B — Long and Short-Run Equilibrium

Animated Adjustment to Long-Run Equilibrium

Watch the AD-AS diagram, output Y, and price level P evolve in calendar time after a shock. The economy spends years at the short-run point B while expected prices slowly adjust — this lab makes that duration visible.

Instructions

The economy starts at long-run equilibrium A (Y = 50, P = 50). At time t = 0, you apply a shock. The economy jumps to a short-run equilibrium B, then slowly drifts toward a new long-run equilibrium C as the expected price level E[P] catches up with the actual price level. The speed of that catch-up is set by the adjustment speed control.

  1. Pick a shock. Demand shocks shift AD; temporary supply shocks shift SRAS up briefly; permanent supply shocks shift both SRAS and LRAS.
  2. Pick an adjustment speed. The model is the same; only the calendar time to reach C changes.
  3. Press Play. The simulation runs in calendar time. Use Pause and Step to inspect intermediate states. Reset returns to A.
  4. Compare runs. Turn on the overlay to keep a slow, medium, and fast trajectory on the time-series panels side by side.
Your system requests reduced motion. The animation is paused — use Step to advance one time-step at a time, or Play to start the animation explicitly.
