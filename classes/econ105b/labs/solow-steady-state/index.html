Solow Model: Steady State & Golden Rule

Solve a randomized Solow economy all the way through — steady state, golden rule, and long-run growth rates — and watch the Solow diagram get built one answer at a time.

Want to see the diagram move first? Open the Solow Model: Diagram Explorer — drag the five parameters and watch the steady state, the transition path, and the golden-rule trade-off respond.

Instructions

You are handed a Solow economy with a Cobb–Douglas production function and values for the capital share, the depreciation rate, population growth, technological progress, and the savings rate. Your job is to take it apart: find where the economy settles, find the savings rate that would make its residents best off, and say what actually grows in the long run and what does not.

Questions unlock one at a time. You must commit an answer to a question before the next question opens and before any help for that question becomes available — that is the point. The diagram on the right is drawn in layers as your answers come in.

You will be asked to answer the following questions:

  1. Production function. Identify the per-effective-worker production function implied by the aggregate one.
  2. Break-even investment. Give the coefficient on capital in the transition equation — the investment needed just to hold capital per effective worker steady.
  3. Steady-state capital. Solve the transition equation for its steady state.
  4. Steady-state output. Feed that capital stock through the production function.
  5. Steady-state consumption. Find what is left after saving — by either of two routes.
  6. Golden-rule savings rate. Find the savings rate that maximizes steady-state consumption.
  7. Position and cost. Say whether this economy saves above, below, or at the golden rule, and compute how much consumption that position costs.
  8. Growth accounting. Give the long-run growth rates of output per effective worker, output per worker, and aggregate output.

For each question, enter your answer and click Check Answer. After you submit, a three-step help ladder opens — Hint, then Formula, then Worked solution. Using help is recorded but never penalized. Once all eight are submitted, a correction pass opens so you can revise before submitting final answers.
