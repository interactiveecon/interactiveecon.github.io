ECON 105B — Review of Aggregate Demand

Policy Classifier

Read each policy scenario, then classify it: Is it fiscal or monetary? Expansionary or contractionary? Which curve shifts? What happens to AD and r?

Instructions

Each round shows a news-style policy headline. Read it, then answer five classification questions.

  1. Question 1 — Type of policy. Is the action fiscal (changing G or T) or monetary (changing the money supply M)?
  2. Question 2 — Stance. Does it stimulate the economy (expansionary) or restrain it (contractionary)?
  3. Question 3 — Which curve shifts. Fiscal policy shifts IS; monetary policy shifts LM. Pick the direction too.
  4. Question 4 — Effect on AD. Does AD shift, or is there only a movement along AD? (Policy shifts AD — only a change in P moves along it.)
  5. Question 5 — Effect on r. The interest rate r is determined endogenously in the money market. It can rise, fall, or stay unchanged. Think about what happens to money demand and money supply after the shock plays out.
