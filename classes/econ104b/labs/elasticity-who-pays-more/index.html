ECON 104B — Price Discrimination

Elasticity → Who Pays More?

Before computing anything, predict which of two segmented markets gets charged the higher price. Then open the sandbox and watch the inverse elasticity rule — P = [E / (1 + E)] × MC — set the markup in each market.

Instructions

This lab has two parts. The first is a short prediction quiz that forces you to commit to an answer before the math is in front of you; the second is an interactive sandbox that lets you see the rule in action across textbook scenarios.

You will be asked to answer the following questions:

  1. Who pays more? Given the elasticities of two customer groups, predict which group a profit-maximizing firm charges the higher price.
  2. What happens to the markup? Predict how the optimal markup over marginal cost changes as a segment's demand becomes more or less elastic.
  3. Put numbers on it. Apply the formula P = [E / (1 + E)] × MC to compute optimal prices and price ratios across segments.

After the quiz unlocks, the sandbox lets you slide each segment's elasticity (and a shared marginal cost) and watch the demand curve, marginal revenue curve, optimal price, and producer surplus respond live in two side-by-side graphs.
