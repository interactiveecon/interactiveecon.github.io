ECON 104B — Price Discrimination

Build Your Own Segmentation Strategy

Step into the role of a manager designing a price-discrimination strategy. Pick which observable attributes to segment on, set prices for each segment, and see how the firm's profit and the chapter's two requirements play out.

Instructions

For each scenario you will: read the business setup and the latent customer types, choose one or more attributes to segment on, set a price for each resulting segment, and run the simulation to see your profit and any strategy problems.

You will be asked to answer the following questions:

  1. Direct vs. indirect attributes. Identify which of the attributes you used are observable at the point of purchase (direct / 3rd-degree) and which require customer choice (indirect / 2nd-degree).
  2. Incentive compatibility. Recognize when a self-declared menu without verification breaks down.
  3. The two requirements. Identify which of the chapter's two prerequisites — market power, or preventing resale — is at risk in each setting.

You can rerun the simulation as many times as you like — only the first submission of each scored prompt is recorded.
