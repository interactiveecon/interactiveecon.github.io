Why Externalities Cause DWL: Build-Your-Own-Wedge

Watch the deadweight loss triangle appear, one piece at a time. Start with a market that has no externality, then add an external cost (or benefit) and see where private and social interests diverge.

Instructions

This lab is a guided visual primer on why externalities cause deadweight loss. Walk through five build stages — each one adds a single new element to the graph — and watch the DWL triangle emerge.

Controls you can use at any time:

  1. Externality type — toggle between a negative externality (production-side, wedge between private and social supply) and a positive externality (consumption-side, wedge between private and social demand).
  2. Stage — step through the five build stages with Previous / Next, or click any stage badge directly.
  3. External effect size — drag the slider to change the size of the per-unit external cost or benefit. Set it to 0 to see the wedge — and the DWL triangle — collapse to nothing.

At the bottom, answer three multiple-choice questions to check your understanding, then click Submit answers.
