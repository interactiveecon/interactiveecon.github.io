ECON 104B — Public Goods & Common Resources

Tragedy of the Commons: Open Access & Policy Remedies

A shared fishery (rival but nonexcludable). Watch how open access leads to overuse, then apply a Pigouvian fee, a quota, or property rights and see deadweight loss shrink and stock health recover.

Instructions

N identical fishermen share an open-access fishery. Each fisherman picks an effort level ei; total effort is E = Σ ei. As total effort rises, the catch per unit effort falls due to congestion: H(E)/E = α − βE.

Each fisherman's private marginal benefit per unit of their own effort, evaluated at symmetric play, is MPB = p·(α − βE·(N+1)/N). The social marginal benefit is MSB = p·(α − 2βE) — steeper, because it accounts for the congestion every extra unit imposes on the others. Marginal cost is constant: MC = w. With more users (larger N) the MPB curve rotates flatter and its intersection with MC drifts right.

Use the controls to compare regimes:

  1. Adjust the number of fishermen N and watch the open-access total effort drift toward the catch-collapse level.
  2. Choose a policy regime — open access, Pigouvian fee, quota, or sole owner — then adjust its magnitude (where applicable).
  3. Watch the DWL triangle on the left canvas and the comparison bars on the right shrink toward the social optimum.
  4. Answer the four questions once you've explored.
