ECON 104B — Oligopoly

Bertrand Competition with Differentiated Products

Two firms produce close but imperfect substitutes and compete by choosing prices. Derive each firm's best response, solve the differentiated-Bertrand equilibrium, and compare the resulting markup to the homogeneous-good benchmark.

Instructions

You will solve a differentiated-product Bertrand duopoly step by step. Each firm chooses its own price; its quantity demanded depends positively on the rival's price (the products are substitutes) and negatively on its own price.

You will be asked to answer the following:

  1. Total revenue and marginal revenue for each firm, expressed as functions of both prices.
  2. Best response prices BR1 and BR2.
  3. Equilibrium prices, quantities, and profits.
  4. One conceptual question comparing the differentiated-Bertrand price to the homogeneous-good benchmark P = MC.

For each step, enter your answer and click Check Answer. If your answer is incorrect, hints and the worked solution are available.

Problem

Two firms produce differentiated products and compete by setting prices. Each firm's quantity demanded depends on its own price and its rival's price, and each firm has a constant marginal cost.
