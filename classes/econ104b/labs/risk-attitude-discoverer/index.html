ECON 104B — Uncertainty & Insurance

Risk Attitude Discoverer

Give your certainty equivalent for each of several 50/50 gambles, and we will plot your responses against the 45° line that represents risk neutrality.

Instructions

You will see a series of 50/50 gambles. For each one, enter the certain dollar amount that would make you indifferent between receiving it for sure and playing the gamble. That number is your certainty equivalent (CE).

You will work through the following steps:

  1. State your CE for each gamble. Fill in every box, then submit them all together.
  2. Read the verdict. Your CEs are plotted against the 45° line CE = EV. Dots below the line indicate risk aversion for that gamble; dots above indicate risk loving; dots on the line indicate risk neutrality.
  3. Answer three comprehension questions on Jensen's inequality, the certainty equivalent, and the risk premium.

For the comprehension questions, pick an option and click Check Answer. If you're wrong, you can revise your choice and resubmit — your initial and final answers are recorded separately.

Your gambles

A fair coin is flipped for each gamble. If it lands heads you receive the high payoff; if tails, the low payoff. Enter your certainty equivalent (CE) for each gamble in the box on the right.
