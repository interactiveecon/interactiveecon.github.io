ECON 104B — Oligopoly

Cournot & Quantity Leadership

Two firms compete by choosing quantities. Solve the simultaneous-move Cournot equilibrium, then re-solve the same market when one firm moves first as a Stackelberg quantity leader.

Instructions

You will solve the same duopoly market under two different timing assumptions. Each firm has its own linear total cost function, and the two firms together face a single linear inverse demand curve. Firms compete in quantities — each chooses how much to produce, and the market price is determined by total output.

You will be asked to answer the following:

  1. Cournot model (firms move simultaneously). Derive each firm's best response function, then find the Cournot quantities, market price, and profits.
  2. Quantity leadership (Firm 1 is the Stackelberg leader). Substitute Firm 2's best response into demand to find Firm 1's residual demand, then derive the leader's optimal quantity, the follower's response, the market price, and each firm's profit.

For each step, enter your answer and click Check Answer. If your answer is incorrect, hints and the worked solution are available.
