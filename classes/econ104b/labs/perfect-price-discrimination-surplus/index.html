Perfect Price Discrimination — Surplus Visualizer

Toggle between single-price monopoly, perfect competition, and first-degree (perfect) price discrimination to see how the same demand and cost curves produce dramatically different distributions of surplus — and why an efficient outcome is not necessarily a good outcome for consumers.

Instructions

This lab compares three pricing regimes on the same linear demand curve and constant marginal cost. Use the regime buttons to switch between them, and watch the shaded surplus regions and the numerical totals update in real time. Use the sliders to confirm the comparisons hold for different parameterizations.

You will then be asked to answer four conceptual questions:

  1. Which regime maximizes total surplus?
  2. Under which regime does consumer surplus disappear?
  3. How does the quantity sold under first-degree price discrimination compare with single-price monopoly?
  4. Does an efficient outcome mean consumers are well off?

Click a choice to lock in your answer for each question.
