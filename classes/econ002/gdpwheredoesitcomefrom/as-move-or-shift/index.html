ECON 002 — Aggregate Supply

Move Along or Shift?

Decide whether each event moves the economy along the SRAS, shifts the SRAS, or shifts the LRAS, and explain why using the real wage and MPL. Then practice the firm's hiring decision with a worker-by-worker table.

Instructions
