The Economy's Influence on the Government Budget

  • Automatic Stabilizer
    • Automatic : Revenue and expenditure items in the federal budget that automatically change with the state of the economy in such a way as to stabilize GDP.
      • insurance
      • Income tax
        • drag: The negative effect on the economy that occurs when average tax rates increase because tax-payers have moved into higher income brackets during an expansion.
      • assistance/ enrollment
    • Automatic stabilizers mean that government spending will increase and taxes will decrease during a recession.
      • This will cause the government's budget deficit to increase.
  • -Employment Budgets
    • Full-employment budget: What the federal budget would be if the economy were producing at the level of output that occurs at the natural rate of unemployment.
      • deficit: The deficit that remains when we are at the natural rate of unemployment.
      • deficit: The deficit that occurs because of a downturn in the business cycle.
