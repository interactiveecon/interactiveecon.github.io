1. What is Economics?

Economics

The study of how individuals, business, and governments make the best decision, given their scarce resources.

Scarcity

The resources needed to fulfill our wants are limited.

  • What is scarce in your life?
  • What is the solution to scarcity?
Trade-Offs

Using resources to carry out one decision means that there are less resources to carry out other decisions.

BIG QUESTION: How do economists determine the best allocation of resources?

3. Economics Overview

3.1 Types of Economics

Microeconomics

Studies how households and firms interact in markets and make choices.

Macroeconomics

The study of the economy as a whole and considers total output, employment, and the price level in the economy.

Macroeconomics Questions

  • What causes recessions?
  • What role does Congress and the President have in maintaining economic stability?
  • What is the Federal Reserve? How do the actions of the Federal Reserve impact the economy?
  • What causes inflation? What can be done to reduce inflation?
  • What causes unemployment? What can be done to reduce unemployment?
  • How can problems in the housing market spread to the rest of the economy?
  • Where does economic growth come from?
  • What is the trade deficit? How does it affect a country's well-being? Do tariffs reduce the trade deficit?

4. The Business Cycle

Recession

A period of economic decline during which trade and industrial activity are reduced, generally identified by a fall in GDP in two successive quarters.

Expansion

A period of economic growth during which trade and industrial activity are increasing.
