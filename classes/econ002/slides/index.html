Dynamic Lecture Slides

The figures from each unit's notes, on their own and sized for the lecture hall. Nothing but the diagram and its title — no prose to read from the back of the room. Arrow keys move between figures, the space bar walks each animation forward one step at a time, and F goes full screen.
