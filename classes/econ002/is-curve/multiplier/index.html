ECON 002 — The Goods Market & Fiscal Policy

The Multiplier

Follow one change in spending round by round to see why output changes by more than the original change, and where the shortcut formula comes from.

Instructions

This lab uses the same economy as lecture: C = 100 + MPC(Y − T), I = 100 − 10r with r = 5, G = 100, and T = 50. With MPC = 0.5, equilibrium output starts at Y = 450.

  1. Choose a change. Pick what changes (investment, government purchases, taxes, or G and T together), how big the change is, and the MPC.
  2. Step through the rounds. Click Next round. Each round follows the chain from lecture: spending changes → unplanned inventories change → firms change production → income changes → consumption changes → and around again. Watch the staircase on the graph and the running total of ΔY.
  3. See the shortcut. When the rounds die out, the lab shows how adding up every round gives the multiplier formula.
  4. Check your understanding. Answer three questions. Click Check Answer; if you're stuck, a hint and the worked solution are available.
